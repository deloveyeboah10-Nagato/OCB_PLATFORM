## Initial Financial Entity Inventory

The following entities are identified as necessary candidates for the OCB Platform v1.0.0 financial entity model.

The inventory distinguishes between core financial/domain entities and lightweight observable reference entities. Inclusion at this stage does not constitute final approval of the entity model. Entity responsibilities, lifecycle, authoritative system, and relationships will be established in subsequent work.

| Entity          | Classification                          | Purpose / Justification                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Institution** | Core domain entity                      | Represents an independent financial institution within the OCB sandbox and establishes the ownership and institutional context of observable financial activity. Required to distinguish Ananse Telecom, SikaCredit, Oman Remit, and their respective financial domains.                                            |
| **Customer**    | Core domain entity                      | Provides the shared simulated identity required to associate observable financial activity with a participant across independent institutional domains and support cross-institution analysis and investigation.                                                                                                    |
| **Wallet**      | Core financial entity                   | Represents the mobile-money value-holding entity required for Ananse Telecom's wallet-based financial activity and associated financial state.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Loan**        | Core financial entity                   | Represents the lending obligation required to model SikaCredit's digital lending activity, including disbursement, repayment, outstanding exposure, and loan-state analysis.                                                                                                                                        |
| **Merchant**    | Lightweight observable reference entity | Identifies the merchant associated with observable merchant-payment activity. Required where merchant-level attribution, investigation, concentration analysis, or suspicious-transaction analysis is needed. OCB does not model the merchant's internal operational or settlement architecture.                    |
| **Agent**       | Lightweight observable reference entity | Identifies the agent associated with observable agent-mediated activity such as cash-in and cash-out. Required where agent-level attribution, investigation, concentration, or geographic analysis is justified. OCB does not model agent float, liquidity, settlement, or other internal institutional operations. |

### Deliberately Excluded from the First-Class Entity Inventory

The following concepts are not currently treated as first-class financial entities for v1.0.0:

| Concept                      | Treatment                       | Reason                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Financial Account**        | Ledger/accounting reference     | Required where appropriate for recording and organising financial consequences within the ledger, but not currently justified as a separate cross-institution business entity. This avoids imposing a generic account architecture across institutions that may have different internal representations.     |
| **Remittance Participant**   | Role within Oman Remit activity | Sender and beneficiary are required remittance concepts, but they do not currently justify a separate OCB entity. They remain participant roles within the Oman Remit domain and may be represented through appropriate observable identifiers or existing participant concepts where required for analysis. |
| **Borrower / Loan Receiver** | Customer role                   | A separate borrower entity is not currently justified. A Customer may participate in SikaCredit lending activity as the borrower/loan recipient.                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Sender**                   | Remittance role                 | A separate sender entity is not currently justified by v1.0.0 requirements. Sender identity may be represented through the appropriate observable participant/customer reference where required.                                                                                                             |
| **Beneficiary**              | Remittance role                 | A separate beneficiary entity is not currently justified by v1.0.0 requirements. Beneficiary identity may be represented through the appropriate observable participant/customer reference where required.                                                                                                   |

### T01 Boundary

This inventory identifies the financial entities required to proceed with the domain entity-definition programme. It does not establish physical database tables, schemas, columns, keys, or implementation structures.

The inventory is intentionally subject to challenge during **WP-1.2-T03 — Identify unnecessary entities**. An entity should remain in the final model only where its business purpose and contribution to approved v1.0.0 financial or intelligence requirements can be demonstrated.

The distinction between a core financial/domain entity and a lightweight observable reference entity is intentional. OCB must obtain sufficient observable information to support financial intelligence and investigation without reproducing the internal operational architecture of the independent institutions.
