### Ananse Telecom

| Field                        | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Term**                     | Ananse Telecom                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Definition**               | An independent simulated financial institution representing the mobile money / electronic money domain within the OCB Platform v1.0.0. Ananse Telecom operates the simulated mobile-money ecosystem and is authoritative for the financial state arising from its own simulated financial activity.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Context**                  | Ananse Telecom is one of the independent institutional domains observed by the OCB Platform. Its domain covers simulated mobile-money financial activity and the associated operational financial information made observable to OCB through the defined sandbox observation boundary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **System / Domain**          | Ananse Telecom — Mobile Money / Electronic Money                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Ownership**                | Ananse Telecom owns the operational meaning and financial consequences of activity within its simulated domain. OCB may observe defined information originating from Ananse Telecom but does not become the owner or operational source of truth for Ananse's financial state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Related Concepts**         | Mobile money; electronic money; wallet; financial event; cash-in; cash-out; P2P transfer; merchant payment; operational financial state; ledger; OCB observable information; observation boundary; financial intelligence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Financial Significance**   | The domain represents simulated mobile-money financial activity including cash-in, cash-out, P2P transfers, and merchant payments. Financial events originating within the Ananse domain produce the authoritative financial consequences and operational state against which subsequent analytical representations are derived.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Analytical Significance**  | Observable Ananse financial activity provides the basis for later analysis of transaction behaviour, transaction velocity, wallet activity, liquidity-related conditions, concentration, anomalous activity, institutional monitoring, and regulatory investigation. These analytical uses do not alter the underlying financial truth of the Ananse domain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Implementation Reference** | To be established by subsequent Ananse operational-data and financial-event implementation work. No physical database object is defined by this glossary entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Status**                   | **Defined**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Notes / Boundary**         | Ananse Telecom is institutionally independent from OCB. Information originating within Ananse is not automatically OCB-observable; only information falling within the defined OCB sandbox observation boundary is available to the OCB analytical environment. The observation boundary is a domain and governance concept and does not imply implementation of APIs, Kafka, event buses, streaming infrastructure, or other institutional integration mechanisms in v1.0.0. OCB analytical representations remain derived representations and must not replace Ananse's authoritative operational financial state. Internal Ananse production mechanisms are intentionally not defined where they are outside the approved v1.0.0 scope. |
