### Inter-Institution Relationships

| Field                        | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Term**                     | Inter-Institution Relationships                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Definition**               | The controlled conceptual relationships between the independent simulated institutional domains represented in the OCB Platform, through which financial activity originating in one institution may be observed and analytically correlated with activity originating in another institution without creating operational coupling between those institutions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Context**                  | The concept applies to cross-institution financial intelligence within the OCB sandbox. Ananse Telecom, SikaCredit, and Oman Remit remain independently operated simulated institutional domains, while OCB may correlate information made observable from those domains to understand shared customer activity, financial behaviour, exposure, or other regulatory intelligence questions.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **System / Domain**          | OCB Platform — Cross-Institutional Observation and Intelligence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| **Ownership**                | Each institution retains ownership of its own operational financial activity and authoritative financial state. OCB owns the analytical interpretation performed within its regulatory intelligence environment, but does not acquire operational ownership of the source institutions' financial systems or states.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Related Concepts**         | Ananse Telecom; SikaCredit; Oman Remit; institutional ownership; customer; shared simulation identity; OCB observable information; observation boundary; cross-institution analysis; financial intelligence; regulatory intelligence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Financial Significance**   | Cross-institution relationships allow financial activity associated with a common simulated customer or other justified analytical relationship to be considered across institutional domains. This may reveal financial outcomes or patterns that cannot be understood adequately by examining an individual institution in isolation.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Analytical Significance**  | The concept supports cross-institution analysis such as correlating lending activity at SikaCredit with mobile-money activity at Ananse Telecom, or examining remittance activity in relation to other observable financial activity. Such analysis is performed from OCB-observable information and does not require the institutions themselves to operate as a coupled system.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Implementation Reference** | To be established by subsequent identity, cross-domain data-model, and analytical implementation work. No physical integration mechanism is defined by this glossary entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Status**                   | **Defined**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| **Notes / Boundary**         | Inter-institution relationships are analytical and observational relationships, not necessarily operational relationships. Ananse Telecom, SikaCredit, and Oman Remit remain independent simulated institutions. The v1.0.0 sandbox uses a controlled shared simulation identity to permit justified cross-institution correlation; this does not represent production-grade national identity resolution or institutional integration. OCB must not infer operational data sharing, direct system connectivity, common internal systems, or real-world institutional dependencies merely because information from separate domains can be correlated analytically. |
