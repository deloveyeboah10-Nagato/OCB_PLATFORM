### SikaCredit

| Field                        | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Term**                     | SikaCredit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Definition**               | An independent simulated financial institution representing the digital lending domain within the OCB Platform v1.0.0. SikaCredit is responsible for the simulated origination, management, and financial activity associated with digital loans within its institutional domain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Context**                  | SikaCredit is an independent institutional domain observed by the OCB Platform. Its domain represents simulated digital lending activity and the associated operational financial information made observable to OCB through the defined sandbox observation boundary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **System / Domain**          | SikaCredit — Digital Lending                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Ownership**                | SikaCredit owns the operational meaning and financial consequences of lending activity within its simulated domain. OCB may observe defined information originating from SikaCredit but does not become the owner or operational source of truth for SikaCredit's lending state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Related Concepts**         | Digital lending; loan; borrower; principal; disbursement; repayment; outstanding balance; loan state; financial event; OCB observable information; observation boundary; credit intelligence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| **Financial Significance**   | The domain represents simulated digital lending activity, including loan origination, disbursement, repayment, and the resulting loan financial state. Financial events originating within the SikaCredit domain provide the basis for the authoritative financial consequences and operational lending state represented within the sandbox.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Analytical Significance**  | Observable SikaCredit lending activity provides the basis for later analysis of lending activity, repayment behaviour, outstanding exposure, delinquency/default-related conditions, credit exposure, portfolio behaviour, institutional monitoring, and regulatory investigation. These analytical uses do not alter the underlying financial truth of the SikaCredit domain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Implementation Reference** | To be established by subsequent SikaCredit operational-data and lending implementation work. No physical database object is defined by this glossary entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Status**                   | **Defined**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Notes / Boundary**         | SikaCredit is institutionally independent from OCB and from the other simulated financial institutions. Information originating within SikaCredit is not automatically OCB-observable; only information falling within the defined OCB sandbox observation boundary is available to the OCB analytical environment. The observation boundary does not imply implementation of APIs, Kafka, event buses, streaming infrastructure, or other institutional integration mechanisms in v1.0.0. OCB analytical representations remain derived representations and must not replace SikaCredit's authoritative operational lending state. Internal SikaCredit production mechanisms are intentionally not defined where they are outside the approved v1.0.0 scope. |
