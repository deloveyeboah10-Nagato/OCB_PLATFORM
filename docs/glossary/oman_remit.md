### Oman Remit

| Field                        | Definition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Term**                     | Oman Remit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Definition**               | An independent simulated financial institution representing the cross-border remittance domain within the OCB Platform v1.0.0. Oman Remit is responsible for the simulated remittance activity and associated financial information represented within its institutional domain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Context**                  | Oman Remit is an independent institutional domain observed by the OCB Platform. Its domain represents simulated cross-border remittance activity and associated information made observable to OCB through the defined sandbox observation boundary.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **System / Domain**          | Oman Remit — Cross-Border Remittance                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Ownership**                | Oman Remit owns the operational meaning and financial consequences of remittance activity within its simulated domain. OCB may observe defined information originating from Oman Remit but does not become the owner or operational source of truth for Oman Remit's financial state.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Related Concepts**         | Remittance; sender; beneficiary; originating institution; receiving institution; origin country; destination country; currency; exchange rate; settlement; remittance status; cross-border flow; OCB observable information; observation boundary; financial intelligence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Financial Significance**   | The domain represents simulated cross-border remittance activity, including remittance inflows, currency conversion, external settlement activity, and cross-border financial flows established within the OCB Platform. These activities provide the financial events and resulting information from which the domain's operational financial state is derived.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Analytical Significance**  | Observable Oman Remit activity provides the basis for later analysis of cross-border flows, remittance behaviour, currency-related activity, geographic concentration, settlement-related conditions, anomalous activity, institutional monitoring, and regulatory investigation. These analytical uses do not alter the underlying financial truth of the Oman Remit domain.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Implementation Reference** | To be established by subsequent Oman Remit operational-data and remittance implementation work. No physical database object is defined by this glossary entry.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **Status**                   | **Defined**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Notes / Boundary**         | Oman Remit is institutionally independent from OCB and from the other simulated financial institutions. Information originating within Oman Remit is not automatically OCB-observable; only information falling within the defined OCB sandbox observation boundary is available to the OCB analytical environment. The observation boundary does not imply implementation of APIs, SWIFT connectivity, payment networks, settlement infrastructure, streaming platforms, or other institutional integration mechanisms in v1.0.0. OCB analytical representations remain derived representations and must not replace Oman Remit's authoritative operational financial state. Internal Oman Remit production mechanisms are intentionally not defined where they are outside the approved v1.0.0 scope. |
